Runeweaver's Chase

Support and privacy for the iPhone, iPad and Android game.

Support

Something broken, something stuck, or a question about the game? Email vpradeep@gmail.com and describe what happened. Please say which device you are playing on, and what you were doing when it went wrong. Replies usually come within a couple of days.

Progress and settings are kept on your own device. Uninstalling the game removes them, and there is no account to recover.

Privacy policy

Runeweaver's Chase does not collect, store, or share any personal information. The game runs entirely on your device, requests no permissions, and makes no network connections. Your settings and progress are saved in the app's own private storage on your device and are removed when you uninstall the game. If you have questions, contact vpradeep@gmail.com.

In more detail:

  • No accounts, no sign-in, and no personal details are asked for.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no third-party SDKs.
  • No network access of any kind, so nothing can leave the device.
  • No access to the camera, microphone, photos, contacts or location.
  • The only thing stored is the game's own settings and score, in the app's private storage, deleted with the app.

This policy covers both the iPhone and iPad version and the Android version of the game. If it ever changes, the change will be published on this page.
