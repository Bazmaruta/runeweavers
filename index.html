Runeweaver's Chase

Support and privacy for the iPhone, iPad and Android game.

Support

Something broken, something stuck, or a question about the game? Email vpradeep@gmail.com and describe what happened. Please say which device you are playing on, and what you were doing when it went wrong. Replies usually come within a couple of days.

Progress and settings are kept on your own device. Uninstalling the game removes them, and there is no account to recover.

Privacy policy

Runeweaver's Chase does not collect, store, or share any personal information of its own. The game runs on your device, asks for no permissions, and has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. Your settings and progress are saved in the app's own private storage on your device and are removed when you uninstall the game. If you have questions, contact vpradeep@gmail.com.

There is one exception, and it is optional: on iPhone and iPad the game can send Apple's Game Center the coins you collected and the achievements you earned. That is the whole of it, and it is described under Game Center below.

In more detail:

  • No account of our own, and no personal details are asked for.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no third-party SDKs.
  • No access to the camera, microphone, photos, contacts or location.
  • The only thing stored is the game's own settings and score, in the app's private storage, deleted with the app.
  • The only thing sent anywhere is the Game Center score and achievements below, and only to Apple.

Game Center

The iPhone and iPad version can use Apple's Game Center for its leaderboard and its achievements. It is optional: nothing is sent unless you are signed in to Game Center on your device.

When you are signed in, the game sends Apple two things — the number of coins you collected in a chase, and which achievements you have earned. Apple shows those on its leaderboard under your Game Center nickname, so other players can see that name alongside your score. The nickname is Apple's, not ours: the game never reads, stores or keeps your nickname, or any account identifier.

Apple handles that information under Apple's privacy policy. It is not sent anywhere else, and we do not keep a copy of it.

If you are not signed in to Game Center, or you play the Android version, the game makes no network connections at all and none of this applies.

Children

The game asks for no personal details and has no accounts of its own, so no personal information is collected from anyone, children included. Game Center sign-in is optional and is governed by your device's own parental controls and by Apple's privacy policy.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the foot of this page changes with it, and this page is always the current version.
